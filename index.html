Moringa powder kya hai?

Moringa (सहजन / drumstick) ke patton ko sukha kar banaya gaya fine powder, jo traditionally Indian diet ka hissa raha hai.

Kitna lena chahiye?

Aam taur par 1 teaspoon (3–5g) rozana. Pack par diye directions follow karein.

Taste kaisa hai?

Halka earthy, green-tea jaisa. Smoothie ya dal mein mix karne par pata bhi nahi chalta.

Kya koi bhi le sakta hai?

Adults ke liye general dietary use. Pregnant/breastfeeding ho, medication par ho ya koi health condition ho to pehle doctor se salah lein.
